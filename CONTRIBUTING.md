# Contributing

Contributions are welcome! feather is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE), and by contributing you agree that your work will be licensed under the same terms.

## Reporting bugs and requesting features

Bug reports and feature requests are welcome as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/exalynt/feather/issues). Please include as much detail as you can, such as steps to reproduce, expected vs. actual behavior, and relevant environment details.

## Submitting changes

1. Fork the repo.
2. Create a branch for your change.
3. Make your changes, with clear, focused commits.
4. Push your branch to your fork.
5. Open a pull request against `main` describing your change.

Please keep pull requests focused on a single change to make them easier to review.
